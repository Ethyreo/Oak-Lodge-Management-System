# Oak Lodge Management System: Master Prompt

> "What if property management was as intuitive as navigating a map?"

## The Hook
Managing a complex property with dozens of tenants, fluctuating utility bills, and manual receipt generation is a relic of the past. **Oak Lodge Management System** is a modern, high-performance solution built to bridge the gap between physical property layouts and digital financial tracking.

## What is Oak Lodge?
Oak Lodge is an advanced Property Management System (PMS) designed for clarity and speed. It moves beyond static spreadsheets into a dynamic, visual experience.

## Key Highlights to Explore:
- **The Visual Map**: An interactive, SVG-driven building layout that lets you "see" your property status at a glance. Select a unit, and you're instantly in its financial heart.
- **The Hydration Engine**: A robust sync system powered by **Firebase Firestore** that ensures your data is always live, always secure, and always available across devices.
- **The Interactive Ledger**: A master record of every tenant, every rent payment, and every utility bill (Electricity, Water, Garbage), designed for high-density data management.
- **Automated Reporting**: One-click professional PDF receipt generation using `jsPDF`, ensuring tenants receive clear, branded documentation of their payments.

## The Tech Stack
Built on the bleeding edge:
- **Core**: React 19 & Vite 7
- **Styling**: Tailwind CSS 4
- **Backend**: Google Firebase (Firestore)
- **UI**: Framer Motion & Recharts for a premium, "living" interface.

## The Mission
We are currently at **Phase 10**, having moved from local prototypes to a fully operational cloud-synced platform. Our goal is to make property management invisible—letting landlords focus on people, not paperwork.
